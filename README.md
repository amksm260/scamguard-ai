# ScamGuard
 
**An AI safety companion for elderly users — before they click, pay or share.**
 
STOP → CHECK → VERIFY → PROTECT
 
> Submitted for HackWave 3.0, Chameli Devi Group of Institutions (CDGI), Indore.
 
---
 
## The Problem
 
Elderly smartphone users increasingly rely on UPI, digital banking and messaging apps — and are increasingly targeted by scams that impersonate banks, government departments and courier services. These messages are built to create pressure at the exact moment a decision has to be made: *Should I click? Should I pay? Should I share this?*
 
Most people already know scams exist. The real gap is what happens in that moment of pressure, with no simple way to check before acting.
 
## What ScamGuard Does
 
ScamGuard takes a suspicious SMS, WhatsApp message or call transcript and returns one of three plain-language verdicts:
 
| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **LOW RISK** | No strong warning signs found. |
| 🟠 **REVIEW** | Some warning signs, not enough to be sure — verify independently. |
| 🔴 **HIGH RISK** | Strong warning signs — do not click, pay, reply or share. |
 
Every verdict comes with **why** it was flagged and **what to do next**, in plain English or Hindi, with a read-aloud option — not a jargon-heavy security score.
